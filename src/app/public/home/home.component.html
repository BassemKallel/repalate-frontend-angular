4 quick steps to use our services

  • Download the App

    Get Replate on your phone to start donating, volunteering, or receiving food.

  • Sign Up

    Create your profile and choose your role: donor, volunteer, or beneficiary.

  • Post or Claim Food

    Donors list surplus food, and NGOs or individuals can request what they need.

  • Collect & Share Impact

    Pick up, deliver, or receive food and track the difference you've made.
